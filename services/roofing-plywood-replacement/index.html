Every detail matters.

Roofing plywood supports the finished roof system. Over time it can deteriorate or rot. We inspect the decking and explain whether replacement is needed before proceeding with the new shingles; a new roof does not automatically mean all the plywood needs replacing.

How we can help

  • Inspection of existing roof decking
  • Identification of deteriorated plywood
  • Replacement where required before roofing
