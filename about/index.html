MEET THE ROOFING MASTER

A family business.
A professional standard.

We are a family-owned and operated roofing business, working with homeowners and commercial customers across Hamilton, Halton, Niagara and nearby communities.

With more than four decades of experience, our team focuses on quality roof replacement and careful installation. Our professional installers carry WSIB coverage and fall-arrest certificates.

We plan each project around the agreed schedule and budget, with respect for your family and property throughout the work.

EXPERIENCE ACROSS YOUR ROOFLINE

From the structure
to the finishing details.

Our services include shingle replacement, new plywood decking, flat and low-slope roofs, tar and gravel roofing, blown-in insulation, and skylight and ventilation installation.

We serve Hamilton, Ancaster, Dundas, Burlington, Oakville and the wider Halton and Niagara regions. Tell us about your property and we’ll help you plan the next step.
